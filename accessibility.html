Accessibility

Professional service for everyone.

Our Commitment

Urban Tree Fellers is committed to ensuring that our digital presence is accessible to everyone, regardless of ability. We believe that accessibility is a core part of professional service and we strive to make our online information inclusive for all our clients and community members.

Our Effort

We are working to ensure that our website conforms to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1. We have implemented several key features to improve the user experience, including:

  • Responsive design that adapts to various devices and screen sizes.
  • Clear, high-contrast headings for improved readability.
  • Semantic HTML structure to assist screen reading software.

A Note on our Progress:

As a specialized service provider, we focus our primary resources on safety and precision in the field. While we strive for full digital compliance, we operate on a "best effort" approach and continuously improve our site based on client feedback.

Feedback & Support

If you are experiencing any difficulty accessing our content or have suggestions on how we can improve our digital accessibility, please let us know.
